# Pixel Clash: things to ask your AI helper

You don't write any code. Just tell your AI helper (Claude or Codex) what you want, in your own words!

## First time

- "Hi! My player name is **___** and my PIN is **____**."
- "Are you connected to Pixel Clash?"

## Make a unit

- "Make me a fast ninja soldier that runs at the closest enemy and slashes it. Make it green."
- "Make a tower that shoots really far but slowly. Purple, please."
- "Make a tiny robot that's hard to block."
- "Make my soldiers wait near my towers until enemies come close."

## Walls and factories

- "Build me a wall to protect my towers."
- "Make a super-tough castle wall that's still cheap."
- "Make a factory that sends minions to the middle."
- "Make a factory that sends lots of tiny robots at the strongest enemy."
- "Make a factory that builds archers to guard my towers."
- "Make a factory that floods the map with minions."
- "Make a runner factory that saves up 4 and then rushes the weakest enemy."
- "Make brutes that smash through walls."
- "My soldiers keep getting stuck behind walls. Make them smash through."

## Fire, ice, lightning and helpers

- "Make an ice tower that slows everything near my base."
- "Make a medic that heals my tanks."
- "Fire archers from my factory."
- "Give my soldiers lightning so they zap the enemies standing next to each other."
- "Make a drummer that makes my soldiers hit harder."
- "Build a medic soldier called Patch with no damage, heal 96 and reach 120. It follows my tank, puts out fires first and then heals whoever is hurt most. How many can I afford next to two Half Tanks?"
- "Make a fast scout that dodges arrows." (shots at fast-moving units can miss)
- "What can my units see during a battle?"

## Change how it looks

- "Draw my ninja with a red headband and make it bigger."
- "Give my tower a wizard hat and a glowing crystal."

## Make it better

- "My unit costs too much. Make it cheaper but still strong."
- "Why did my units stand still last battle? Fix them."
- "How much does my army cost, and how much money is left?"

## Remember

- Send units in the **Plan** phase. Then **pick** them and **place** them on the game page.
- If your AI helper says it can't send your unit yet, tell it **"The Plan phase started!"** when the game page says Plan.
- You have **$10,000** each round for up to **12 fighters**, up to **4** kinds of soldiers and **4** kinds of buildings (towers, walls, factories), and a unit can cost up to **$3,000**. The 4th copy of the same unit costs more, and the 5th more again.
- Walls block your own units too: leave a gap! And walls (or helpers that do no damage) alone can't win: you always need a unit that attacks.
- Battles last 2 minutes. After 75 seconds a safe circle shrinks. Units outside it get hurt!
