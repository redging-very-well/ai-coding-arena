# Pixel Clash: things to ask Claude

You don't write any code. Just tell Claude what you want, in your own words!

## First time

- "Hi! My player name is **___** and my PIN is **____**."
- "Are you connected to Pixel Clash?"

## Make a unit

- "Make me a fast ninja soldier that runs at the closest enemy and slashes it. Make it green."
- "Make a tower that shoots really far but slowly. Purple, please."
- "Make a tiny robot that's hard to block."
- "Make my soldiers wait near my towers until enemies come close."

## Change how it looks

- "Draw my ninja with a red headband and make it bigger."
- "Give my tower a wizard hat and a glowing crystal."

## Make it better

- "My unit costs too much. Make it cheaper but still strong."
- "Why did my units stand still last battle? Fix them."
- "Make a healer... wait, can units heal? Find out and make the best support unit you can."

## Remember

- Send units in the **Plan** phase. Then **pick** them and **place** them on the game page.
- If Claude says it can't send your unit yet, tell it **"The Plan phase started!"** when the game page says Plan.
- You have **$1,000** each round, up to **3** kinds of units, and a unit can cost up to **$500**.
- After 50 seconds a safe circle shrinks. Units outside it get hurt!
